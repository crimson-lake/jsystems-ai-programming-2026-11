# Complaint Policy (example, synthetic)

> Synthetic example document for the Hardware Service Decision Copilot PoC. It belongs to a fictional electronics retailer ("the Store"). It is loosely based on the Polish Consumer Rights Act rules on non-conformity of goods with the contract (in force since 1 January 2023) but is **not legal advice** and must be reviewed by the business before any real use.

## C1. Scope
C1.1. This policy covers complaints about goods that are **faulty, defective or not as described** (non-conformity of goods with the contract).
C1.2. It applies to consumers. Manufacturer warranties are separate and are not assessed by the Copilot.

## C2. Liability period
C2.1. The Store is liable for non-conformity that becomes apparent within **2 years from delivery** of the goods.
C2.2. If the non-conformity becomes apparent within those 2 years, it is **presumed** to have existed at delivery, unless the Store proves otherwise (for example, the damage was clearly caused after delivery by the user).
C2.3. A complaint about goods bought **more than 2 years** before the complaint date is outside the Store's liability period and must be rejected under this policy. The agent may suggest checking the manufacturer's warranty.

## C3. Covered defects (likely to be accepted)
- manufacturing or material defects: dead pixels, backlight bleed above normal, failed buttons, ports that stopped working without visible force damage, battery capacity loss far beyond normal for the device age, cracks appearing without impact marks (e.g. along a hinge),
- defects present on delivery: delivered damaged, missing parts, item different from the order,
- software or firmware faults that make the device unusable and cannot be fixed by a factory reset.

## C4. Excluded damage (likely to be rejected)
- **mechanical damage caused by the user**: impact points, cracked screen with a visible impact center, dents, bent frames, broken-off parts,
- **liquid damage**: corrosion, liquid marks or stains, unless the product is rated waterproof and was used within its rating,
- damage from unauthorized repair or opening the device,
- normal wear and tear consistent with the device age (scratches from daily use, worn coating),
- damage from using non-original or incompatible chargers or accessories, when visible (burnt connector).

## C5. Remedies (order)
C5.1. The customer may first request **repair or replacement**. The Store may choose the other option if the requested one is impossible or disproportionately costly.
C5.2. If repair or replacement is impossible, failed, or would take unreasonably long, the customer may request a **price reduction** or **withdraw from the contract** (refund). Withdrawal is not available when the non-conformity is insignificant; non-conformity is presumed to be significant.
C5.3. The Copilot must not promise a specific remedy. It may say which remedies are possible under this policy.

## C6. Procedure and next steps
C6.1. Accepted complaint: the customer sends the item to the Store service center or brings it to any Store shop, with a description of the defect and proof of purchase. The Store covers shipping costs for accepted complaints.
C6.2. The Store answers a complaint within **14 calendar days** of receiving it. A complaint with no answer in that time is treated as accepted.
C6.3. Every acceptance or rejection given by the Copilot is **preliminary**. The final decision is made by the service team after physical inspection.

## C7. Safety
C7.1. If the photo or description indicates a **swollen battery, burn marks, smoke, a burning smell, sparks or exposed wiring**, the agent must first tell the customer to stop using and stop charging the device, keep it away from flammable materials, and must escalate the case to a specialist regardless of other findings.

## C8. When to escalate to a specialist
- the cause of damage cannot be determined from the photo and the description (could be a defect or user damage),
- the description contradicts the photo,
- any safety case under C7,
- the customer disputes a rejection with new facts that the agent cannot verify from a photo.
