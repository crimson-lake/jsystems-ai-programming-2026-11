# Return Policy (example, synthetic)

> Synthetic example document for the Hardware Service Decision Copilot PoC. It belongs to a fictional electronics retailer ("the Store"). It is loosely based on Polish/EU consumer law but is **not legal advice** and must be reviewed by the business before any real use.

## R1. Scope
R1.1. This policy covers returns of goods that **work correctly** and that the customer no longer wants (change of mind). Faulty or damaged goods are handled by the Complaint Policy, not by this policy.
R1.2. It applies to consumers (natural persons buying outside business activity).

## R2. Return window
R2.1. Statutory right: for purchases made online or by phone, the consumer may withdraw from the contract within **14 calendar days** from the day the goods were delivered, without giving a reason.
R2.2. Store extension: the Store voluntarily accepts returns of goods bought **online or in a Store shop within 30 calendar days** from the purchase date.
R2.3. Day 1 is the day after the purchase date. A return requested on day 31 or later is **outside the window** and must be rejected under this policy. The agent may suggest the Complaint Policy only if the customer reports a defect.

## R3. Condition of the returned item
R3.1. The Store accepts the return with a full refund when the item **can be resold as new**:
- no visible scratches, dents, cracks, stains or discoloration,
- no signs of use (fingerprints on glossy surfaces alone are acceptable; wear marks on keys, ports, hinges, ear cushions or straps are not),
- screen protective film still present where the product shipped with it (smartphones, tablets, monitors, TVs, smartwatches),
- all accessories, cables and manuals included, original packaging present.
R3.2. Testing the item in the way one would test it in a shop (switching on, checking functions) is allowed and is not "use".
R3.3. If the item shows **light signs of use** beyond R3.2 but no damage, the return may still be accepted, but the refund may be **reduced by the loss of value** found during physical inspection. The agent must say that a reduction is possible and must not state an amount.
R3.4. If the item is **damaged** (cracks, dents, liquid marks, broken parts), the return is rejected under this policy.

## R4. Excluded items
R4.1. Headphones, earbuds and other in-ear audio products are returnable **only if the hygiene seal is intact**.
R4.2. Items with software or digital content activated on the customer's account (e.g. a registered smartwatch that cannot be unpaired) must be reset to factory settings and unlinked before return.

## R5. Procedure and next steps
R5.1. Accepted return: the customer packs the item with all accessories, attaches a printed or written return statement and sends it to the Store returns center, or brings it to any Store shop.
R5.2. The Store refunds the price within **14 calendar days** from receiving the withdrawal statement, but may withhold the refund until the item arrives or proof of dispatch is provided.
R5.3. Every return acceptance given by the Copilot is **preliminary**. The final decision is made after physical inspection of the item.

## R6. When to escalate to a specialist
- the photo and the description contradict each other,
- the item category is "Other" and its resale condition cannot be judged,
- the customer claims the item was delivered damaged (route to the Complaint Policy and escalate),
- the purchase date is unclear or disputed.
